## Invariants

  integer accounting and seeded PRNG draw/iteration order determine its
  outcomes. Lint enforces selected cases, not every possible host API.
- Tick iteration snapshots parent IDs before execution, so newborns wait
  until the next tick. Diffusion conserves resources exactly. Probe-energy
  conservation is not asserted by the current suite.
- Snapshot/restore preserves all 14 state fields, PRNG state, insertion
  order, quarantine priority and intervention provenance. Host tests and
  goldens pass; this is not malformed-input or cross-version recovery proof.

  draws. Extinct holds remain an exception to the intended behavior.

  host. Transition-window commands are rejected; persistence-management
  commands are skipped during reconstruction. Rejected commands and step
  replay retain the fidelity defects below.

  swaps, and writer flushes serialize pending appends. These do not imply
  crash-atomic transactions across logs, snapshots, indexes and markers.

  contradicting the architecture/reviver comments. Their population maps
